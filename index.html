Our Story

A Lush Escape
in Rajkot

Step into Arbora — a botanical sanctuary where global flavours meet nature-inspired elegance. Nestled along the 150 Feet Ring Road, we've crafted a world where every corner invites you to slow down and truly savour the moment.

From delicate Italian pastas to soulful Indian curries, our menu spans continents — all crafted with finest pure vegetarian ingredients, presented with a chef's artistry, and served with Rajkot's legendary warmth.
